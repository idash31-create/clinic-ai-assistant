Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Demonstration status: This application is under development and is not currently providing medical services. This policy describes its intended current testing use, not a production clinic deployment.

Who is responsible

Clinic AI Assistant is a demonstration application maintained by its project operator. For questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact aidsol.co@gmail.com. Before any real clinic deployment, this page must be updated with the actual clinic operator's identity and accurate contact details.

Information used in testing

When you interact with the WhatsApp test integration, information may include your WhatsApp phone number, message text, message ID, and basic messaging metadata. If you explicitly request a demonstration booking, it may additionally include the name and date you provide, along with a booking ID and a record of your confirmation. The demonstration portal may contain fictional patient, booking and payment examples.

Do not provide sensitive health information, medical histories, identification documents, or payment card details. If such information is sent inadvertently, contact us to request removal.

Why information is used

Information is used to test message delivery, generate administrative responses, prevent duplicate requests, validate test bookings, troubleshoot errors and improve system reliability. The service is not used to diagnose medical conditions or make clinical decisions.

Services and sharing

Depending on the workflow being tested, WhatsApp messaging is handled through Meta's WhatsApp platform; workflow automation runs in n8n Cloud; text provided for AI replies may be processed by the configured AI model provider via n8n; and demonstration booking records may be stored in Google Sheets. These services may process data under their own applicable terms and privacy notices. Access is limited to those operating the test and authorized service providers where applicable. We do not claim to sell personal data.

Storage and retention

Test data may remain in workflow execution or diagnostic logs, conversation context, the demonstration spreadsheet, and provider systems for the period needed to complete testing and troubleshooting. A fixed retention period has not yet been established for this prototype. Deletion requests are reviewed and handled where the operator has access and control; provider backups or legally required records may not be immediately erasable. Production retention rules must be established before real patient use.

Security and limitations

The prototype uses access-controlled services and limited test access where configured. No internet-connected system can promise perfect security. This testing environment is not approved for real patient medical records.

Your requests

You can ask what test information about you has been recorded, request correction or deletion, or stop participating in testing. Send your request to aidsol.co@gmail.com. See Data Deletion Instructions for details.

Updates

The policy will be updated before connecting a real clinic phone number or processing real patients' information. Material changes will be reflected by a new update date on this page.
