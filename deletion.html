Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 7, 2026

To request deletion of your information from the Clinic AI Assistant test environment, follow these steps:

  1. Email aidsol.co@gmail.com with the subject Clinic AI Assistant – Data Deletion Request.
  2. State whether you used the WhatsApp test or the demonstration portal and provide the phone number or booking reference associated with the test information you want removed. Do not email identification documents or medical details.
  3. The project operator will review the request, may ask for minimal verification when necessary to avoid deleting someone else's records, and will explain the scope of deletion available from the demo workflow and spreadsheet.

What may be deleted

Where accessible, the operator may delete related demonstration patient/booking records and associated test logs under their control. Data controlled by third-party providers or stored in their backups may be subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

Confirmation

You will receive a response by email describing what was handled and any outstanding limitations. No fixed turnaround time is promised for the testing environment.

For broader privacy questions, please read the Privacy Policy.
